# Descriptive Statistics
In the provided dataset, we have 1,200 orders starting from January 2023 till June 2025. On average, orders are valued at $1,054 with the median at $824; thus, the dataset is skewed to the right because most of the orders cost less than the average amount. As for quantities per order, they vary from 1 to 5, and items in cart vary from 1 to 10.
# Distributions
TotalPrice is characterized by right skewness, which means that most orders cost a small amount, and only a few orders cost an unusually large amount of money. This situation occurs often enough in such databases.
Outliers
Only a few orders more expensive than $3,300 were found to be outliers in the dataset. These were the bulk orders, consisting of 5 units. Also, there is one case where the price of the Phone item is lower than the others, specifically $11.39 and is therefore considered an outlier.
# Product Analysis
Printer is the most popular item sold in the database; however, the rest of the products showed equal popularity. Overall, seven product categories are presented in the database.
Order Status
Among all orders, those cancelled were the most common, while the least common were delivered orders.
# Order Status
The order status with the largest number was that of cancelled orders. Orders in the delivered status had the lowest occurrence. This is an issue which may require further analysis to be done.
# Referral Source
Instagram emerged as the most powerful source of customer leads indicating a very effective marketing approach on the social media platform. Direct referrals brought the fewest customers.
# Sales Trend
There was no evident trend of increasing or decreasing monthly sales over the specified time. The months showing the highest revenues were April 2023 and June 2024.
# Seasonal Variation
June had the highest number of orders among all the other months, possibly attributed to high consumer expenditure due to vacation in the summer.
